## Known gaps

**Method (a judgement call for the owner).** The drawing is a vector tracing of the source, not a hand-built drawing: value studies as nested iso-value outlines (0.025 steps) plus traced ink strokes, drawn by code. No pixels or image files are stored or loaded. Pass 3 extends this. There are now two tracings of the designer, the owl and the desk's left part, one per source clip, and ten whole frames of the settle (23.12–23.48) are traced one by one, each as a single flattened drawing. That adds 9.4 MB of data, 11 MB in all for s05. If that breaks the spirit of "drawn by code", the fix-pass-1 procedural drawing is kept in `src/shots/s05/legacy/`.

**The laptop screen is the largest remaining error.**
- **Pure Imagination (24.16–26.68):** σ2 about 10.5 in the screen region, against about 2.8 outside it. It is F3/s07's original page. It has the original "Wonder Camp" mark, not the source's third-party logo. "Imagination" is set in Fraunces, not the source's lettering; I left the source's wordmark lettering untraced on purpose, because it is third-party title art. The fan behind "Pure", the sun face and the props differ in design. Pass 3 fixed the ground colour (it was 2–4 levels too green-yellow) and softened the page slightly. The layout differences remain.
- **sōko (23.12–23.68):** it is F1's page. The source's laptop shows a different rendering of that illustration: dimmer, softer, and with the objects and the figure's pose redrawn. Pass 3 re-fitted where the page sits and its tone, but the illustration itself still differs. σ2 in the screen is about 7.9 at rest and 14–15 during the settle.
- **The file window (23.72–24.12):** an original stand-in, a generic list window, laid out from the source.

**Settle (23.12–23.48).**
- Frames 578–587 are per-frame key tracings, each traced independently of the others. Their fine texture changes a little more from frame to frame than the source's does: the motion-compensated residual is 3.0–3.2 against the source's 2.4–2.8.
- Frame 588 goes back to the warped layers. The 23.48 → 23.52 step carries about 1.4 more texture change than the source's, so the ink reads slightly crisper from there on.
- The page on the screen in those frames is drawn over each key, carried and smeared by the desk's settle grid. It sits about 1 px low at 23.20–23.52.
- The old layered settle path (motion grids, smear, keyboard fill, pale cartons, the extra room blur) is kept. It serves frame 588, and any frame dropped from `KEYS`.

**Hands and forearm.**
