## How it is built

Everything is keyed to the source frame index `k = floor(t·25)`, so each source drawing lands on its own frame.

- **Page.** F1's `sokoPageCanvas({header:false, gaze:"down"})`.
  - F1's own torn hole and rim are painted out. The hole is filled with the measured interior colour.
  - The source's rim is drawn instead as traced strokes. The source redraws the rim twice:
    - rim A up to 85.56;
    - rim B, about 1 px higher, blended in by the 85.60–85.76 dissolve;
    - rim C from 86.16.
  - Once the banner is in, the banner's pale edge fades out over 91.56–91.76 (frames 2289–2294). The rim goes with it.
- **Strip (84.12–85.88).** F1's header content, frame x 193.8–1227.7, with a 5 px pale paper band measured on all four edges.
  - Placed per frame by the ORB similarity track (230–430 inliers).
  - 85.60–85.76 is a dissolve between two copies. Both poses and the weight were fitted jointly per frame.
  - 85.80–85.88 were fitted on the frame.
  - Drawn with a 1 px blur, which is the measured softness of the carried pieces.
- **Banner (86.56–88.68).** F2's `hippieBannerCanvas()` at F1's `HEADER`.
  - Placed by the ORB track, with a 1 px blur while carried.
  - After the slap it trembles until 88.68 (phase correlation of its two halves), then it is part of the page.
- **Cels.** For each source frame the tracer does the following:
  1. Undoes the grade (exact inverse of the finish pass), the camera shake and F1's margin-glow model.
  2. Predicts the background: the clean plate with the strip or banner warped in by the tracks.
  3. Masks what differs. The mask is clip-aware, keeps only components anchored off the strip, fills holes, and grows into the faint dark greys next to the owl.
  4. Traces the foreground as 8–11 nested level regions with one flat pre-grade colour each (contours simplified to 0.6–1.0 px).
  - The motion-streak frames of the last second are traced as translucent light (alpha levels), so they light our page rather than carrying the source page's texture.
  - The shard cut lines are stroked centre lines with measured widths and colours (white top-hat and skeleton).
  - The cels hold only outlines, colours and widths, never pixels. This is the same kind of measurement as `hippie.js` and the s12 strokes.
- **Shake.** Re-measured by sub-pixel phase correlation on the static banner and page: 92.64–93.60 up to ±4 × ±5 px, and 94.88–95.20 up to 5.4 px. The whole frame is offset.
- **Margin glow.** The source's glow above and beside the screen runs brighter than F1's model while bright things sit high on the screen. It was measured pre-grade (source ÷ ours):

  | Stretch | Gain |
  |---|---|
  | Strip up | 1.12 |
  | Banner carried | 1.1 → 1.3 |
  | Banner in | 1.22 |
  | Power-up | 1.15 |
  | End | 1.1 |

## Known gaps (honest)

- **The owl and effects are rotoscoped.** They are per-frame vector traces of the source, not a parametric drawing. Nothing in them can be re-posed, and they only exist for these 326 frames.
- **Posterised look.**
  - Each drawing is 8–11 flat grey levels. Up close the owl's soft feather shading shows as bands and small facets.
  - Texture and ink finer than about 1.5 px are lost: grain, feather hairlines, the thin grey edges of the headphone band.
  - The headphone band is only partly present where it crosses the strip.
  - Adding traced thin strokes measured worse, so they were left out.
- **Contour softness.** The fills are hard-edged (no feathering measured better than any). They are a little crisper at the silhouette and softer inside than the source.
- **Page and banner are not mine.**
  - The framed page is F1's (hole, rim painted out, illustration). The page-region error of about 13.5 is F1's and is the largest part of every frame.
  - The colour banner is F2's `hippieBannerCanvas()`. Its known differences (flat gradient bands, sign and type) show in the header band.
- **Carried strip.** It is F1's header content, not the source's own render of it. Its tone and softness were fitted, but the beanie crop and DO NOT KNOCK sit a few px off.
- **Streak frames at the end.** In the last second (2408, 2411, 2416, 2419, 2424–2426) the streaks are traced as white light with 10 alpha levels. The smears' fine directional texture is lost. The page seen through them is ours, which is right, but it differs from the source's page.
- **Seams.**
  - Where the owl crosses the strip or banner edge, a few frames show a short dark smear along the edge line. The mask takes in some of the pale edge's mismatch there, for example at 87.2 near the plant.
  - Around the strip in 84.1–85.9 there is a faint mismatch where the source's pale paper band is not a clean rectangle.
- **Size and speed.**
  - `cels.js` is 3.9 MB of trace strings, parsed when the module loads. Each cel is decoded to Path2D lazily per frame and cached.
  - The per-frame cost is F1's `drawFramedPage` (about 110 ms) plus 8–11 path fills. It was not timed, because the snapshot clock freezes.
