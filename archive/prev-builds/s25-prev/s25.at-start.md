**How it is built**

This pass replaced the parametric owl, the kit-sprite chaos and the seeded effects.

- **The owl is traced vector drawings, not a hand-built drawing.** Every owl in the shot comes from `s25/tr_*.js`. That covers:
  - the standing owl: 16 drawings, one per distinct source pose;
  - the chaos owls: dive, V-sign ×2, dash, hover ×2;
  - three echo owls;
  - the nine burst frames;
  - the s24 remnant under the opening wipe.

  Each drawing is measured on one source frame and stored as vector data, with no pixels. It has three parts:
  - the figure's outline rings;
  - iso-contours of the ungraded value image (after a 3×3 grey closing) at 12–15 levels, each band filled with its median;
  - centre-lines of the thin dark ink with width and value.

  The data is base64-VLQ integers. `s25/owlTrace.js` redraws it with a 0.45 px blur and tapered ink. This is effectively an automated posterised vectorisation of the source frames at about 0.7 px contour precision, so treat it as such. The tracer is `s25/trace/trace_pose.py`, with the other scripts in `s25/trace/` (see the README there).
- **Which drawing, and where.** A drawing is chosen for each source frame (`timeline.js SCHED`, `CHAOS_OWL`, `ECHO`). It is placed with its own affine, measured by ECC or by a rotation- and scale-searched masked template match on gradients. When a pose is held, the drawing is held and only drifts as measured. Each new pose in the source gets its own drawing. That includes every rise frame 103.44–103.56, 105.48–105.60, the swing at 106.84–106.88 and the wing-up frames 106.92–107.00.
- **Scissors.**
  - During the landing and rise (103.08–103.92) the held pairs are separate traced layers with their own tracks.
  - The thrown pairs (106.92–108.60) are the traced floating pairs from 109.40, rotated and placed by a rotation-searched match per frame. Frames the matcher could not fit continue the spin at 0.87 rad per frame.
- **Camera.**
  - `CAM` is the per-frame affine of the page against 104.60 (ECC on the page outside the owl).
  - The page floats up to 2.5 px.
  - At 108.08 it squashes to y' ≈ 0.982·y + 13.
  - Only the screen moves. The room glow stays where it was, which is measured: the glow rows keep their place and a dark gap opens above the screen's top edge.
- **Light.**
  - `GLOW` is the per-frame screen-glow gain, fitted to the wall above, left and right of the screen.
  - `GAIN` is a σ≈45 px light map of the page in this shot against F1's page, fitted on 13 frames and baked into the page canvas once. The source page reads about 0.75× on the designer, for example.
- **Effects, measured per frame.**
  - Chaos smoke is density iso-contours: the brightness added to the clean page, with thin things removed and owls inpainted (`smoke.js`).
  - Landing dust is 10 gaussians per frame, broken up by a seeded wisp texture.
  - Paper chips are rotated rects detected per frame, 20–160 per frame.
  - Slash arcs are ellipses refitted per frame, with their visible intervals and measured stroke width.
  - The glint is an eight-point star whose reach is measured per frame, from 13 to 80 px.
  - The negative frame at 100.56 is a tone curve measured on that frame.
- **Cracks.** Two traced networks (A up to 103.04, B from 103.08), unchanged from the last pass. The pen was re-fitted to the measured cross-profile: 41 78 141 173 178 142 77 39 at 1 px steps, post-grade, against 21 63 139 178 176 128 53 24 now.
- **Frame snapping.** `draw` snaps to the source frame, `f = ceil(t·25 − 0.001)`. That is exact at the render's k/25 times, and it also pairs `tools/compare.sh` (which seeks on a 1/30 s grid, up to 0.033 s early) with the right source frame.

**Speed**

With warm caches in headless Chrome on the GPU, the median is about 10 ms a frame and p95 about 21 ms. A new drawing or layer costs up to about 200 ms the first time it is built. All caches are dropped in `release()`. Burst layers are near full-frame, so peak cache memory is roughly 100–150 MB.

**Known gaps (honest)**

- **The page is F1's `sokoPageCanvas` plus a measured light map, not the source page.** Its engraving detail differs everywhere: furniture, gear, the designer. This is the largest remaining error in the standing phase. The light map only fixes low-frequency brightness.
- **The traced owl is slightly softer and flatter than the source.**
  - The tone is 12–15 posterised levels with a 0.45 px blur.
  - The ink tracer misses faint strokes and some cross-hatching.
  - Sub-pixel line quality differs.
  - At σ2, pre-grade, the owl interior is about 4.4/255 from the source.
- **Chaos-phase approximations.**
  - The converging echoes (102.38–102.74) reuse the dive and top-right drawings, softened, on keyed blob paths. They are not tracked drawings of those owls.
  - The smoke density is a 6-level contour field, so fine wisps are replaced by a seeded texture.
  - Some slash-arc segments are missed where cracks or owls break the detection, for example the lower-right part of the big ellipse at 102.4–102.6.
  - The main owl's pose is held between traced drawings. Only its affine changes: 100.48–101.48 on one drawing (100.84), 101.88–102.32 on another (102.12). The source redraws it in small ways along the way.
- **Chips.** They are detected, not tracked. A few false positives sit inside headline letters or on owl plumage, and chips hidden under smoke are missed.
- **Spinning scissors.** They are the rigid floating drawing rotated. The blade opening does not vary, and frames the matcher could not fit are extrapolated.
- **Cracks that appear later in the source** (a few around the owl's outline and near the floor after 105.6) are not added. Network B is held from 103.08.
- **The 108.08 squash** is applied to the screen as one affine. The glow is computed before the squash, so near the screen's top edge it is off by a few levels.
- **Dead code.** `s25/fx.js` still contains the older kit-sprite chaos code (sprites, smoke trails, keyed arcs, seeded chips). It is only used as a fallback outside the measured frame ranges, which in practice is only frame 100.48's smoke puffs under the wipe. The superseded parametric owl (`owl.js`, `poses.js`) is kept, unused, in `s25/legacy/`.
