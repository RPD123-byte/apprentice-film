## What changed in this pass (s26c)

1. **No traced owl in the render any more.** Beat A used to draw `s26/poseA.js` with `s26/owlA.js`, a copy of s25's "hips2" drawing. That drawing is an automated posterised vectorisation of a source frame: silhouette, iso-tone bands and ink centre-lines. F2 ruled this kind of data a form of embedding, which the brief forbids.
   - A is now drawn by the new owl painter in B&W inks. It is posed from measurements on 2x gridded crops of the 111.00 frame: eyes, plaster, beak, cups, band, body box, the elbows and hands of the wing-arms, the tail and the feet.
   - `poseA.js` and `owlA.js` are **no longer imported but are still on disk**. They were created in an earlier run, and this run deletes only what it created. You may want to delete `poseA.js`, since it holds the traced data.
   - Cost: in the owl's crop the drawn owl scores 12.6 against 11.2 for the trace, and the whole A frame 7.5 against 7.3.
   - I also tried the kit's fitted `hipsSmug` preset (`owlInk.js`, fitted at 105 s) on this frame. It scored 17.6–18.0 in the crop, with the body too round and the arms too thin, so it was not used.
2. **A new owl painter, `s26/owlColour.js`.** It is used for every owl in the shot: the standing owl in A, the white flying owl in C/D (`INK` palette) and the colour owl in F (`COL` palette).
   - It draws a crown with a heart-shaped cream facial disc, and round eyes with a ring, iris, pupil and catchlights. Other eye states are almond (angry), lidded, shut or happy-closed, with heavy brows.
   - The rest: a hooked beak and a smirk line, the crossed plaster, a slim band with cushioned cups, and a cream fluffy chest with tan shoulders. The wings are grey primaries and secondaries with vanes and a covert row, or folded wings, or feathered arm-wings. It also draws feathered legs with bare toes and dark claws, and perched toes.
   - The inks are the source's colours run back through the film's grade (sampled at 114.3–117.4 s) and then tuned on renders.
   - The wing, body and feet geometry still comes from the rig fits (`s26/rig.js` geometry, `posesC/D/F.js`). `drawRig` is no longer called.
   - The C/D faces are placed by converting the rig's measured landmarks (eyes pinned by the tracked pupils) into the painter's head frame. The skull scale is 0.88, which gives silhouette IoU 0.791 against 0.782 at 1.0.
   - The D feet were measured on 2x grids at 112.88 and 113.60 and move with the body.
3. **F, the colour owl** (`s26/featF.js`, built by `snapshots/s26c-tools/smoothF.py`).
   - **Head.** Centre, size and roll come from the fitted eye pair, median-5 plus 3-tap smoothed.
   - **Face features.** The beak, plaster and both cups are measured per frame as the centres of the orange and peach blobs inside the owl mask, stored in head units.
   - **Refit.** Body, skull size and feet (and the folded wings when perched) are refitted per frame to the source owl mask with the eyes pinned (`fitF.mjs`): silhouette IoU 0.65 → 0.86.
   - **Perched hold.** 117.24–117.52 hold one pose. The source owl is still there: frame differences are at grain level.
   - **Result.** F1 is flat (9.45 against 9.38) and F2 is equal. The painted owl reads closer to the source's cream-and-grey owl, but it does not score better at σ 8.
4. **D ring** (`s26/windowD.js`, `s26/ringD.js`).
   - **Black backing.** The stand-in page tiling is gone. Everything outside the measured colour window at 112.72 is the ring: a black backing (the untraced cells are black shards in the source), moving with the ring's measured zoom.
   - **Cell transforms re-measured.** The 74 traced cells' page similarities had been stored rounded to 0.1 in a, b, d and e, up to ~70 px off across a cell. They were refined around those values with a variance-floored masked NCC against a clean median A stage (`ringrefine.py`).
   - **Tone matching.** Where our own page under that transform was still more than 3 ΔE off, the cell takes the piece of our page whose tones match best (`ringlook.py`). Cell ΔE went 19.8 → 12.0.
   - **More shards.** 28 more shards outside the traced cells were outlined at 112.72 and tone-matched the same way (`ringextra.py`).
5. **C field** (`s26/fieldLook.js`). 179 of the 439 traced cells now show the piece of our B&W or colour page that best matches their tones on 112.00. This replaces their measured-content transform wherever that is better by more than 3 ΔE (`fieldlook.py`). Cell ΔE went 18.1 → 10.9.
6. **E lens shard** (`s26/slabE.js`). The procedural slab texture is replaced, per source frame, by:
   - the shard's measured outline: the convex hull of its mask (low saturation, low fine detail, far from our colour page, owl excluded);
   - the outlines of its lit parts, simplified to 14 px;
   - two measured grey levels;
   - soft seeded streaks at ~80°.

   It is drawn at 1/4 resolution, blurred and softly edged. E went 15.5 → 12.3.
7. **Frame snapping.** `draw` snaps t to `ceil(t·25 − 0.001)/25`, the same rule as s25. That is exact at the render's k/25 times and makes the compare tool's early seeks land on the right beat (111.56: 17.2 → 6.7).

## Known gaps (honest)

- **The owls are simplified.**
  - All three owls are flat painted cartoons.
  - The B&W owl in A/C/D has none of the source's dense ink hatching, and its wing-arms in A are smooth tubes where the source has layered feathers.
  - The colour owl lacks the source's painted texture and soft feather edges.
  - The silhouettes come from fits (IoU 0.79 C/D, 0.86 F), and the flight wings are generic feather fans fitted to outlines.
  - The A owl is posed from one frame and held.
- **The C field and D ring are measured geometry filled with our own pages.**
  - Where a cell's measured transform did not reproduce the source's tones, the cell shows the piece of our page with the closest tones instead. That piece is a different part of the page from what the source shows there: the tones match, but the content in that cell does not.
  - The C field moves rigidly; the source's shards also tumble slightly.
  - D after 113.68 is extrapolated.
  - In D the ring's untraced parts are plain black shards.
- **E.** The lens shard's content is two tones plus seeded streaks, not the source's blurred gear. Its outline is a convex hull per frame, so concave notches are lost.
- **F is bounded by the colour page.** F2's drawn page scores 8.5 on its own. F2 cannot go below that until the page improves.
- **The cut from s25.** s25 still draws its traced owl, so the owl's look changes at 110.52, where the source only un-squashes the frame.
- **Cost.**
  - After the caches are built, each frame is a few canvas passes: C ~440 pattern-filled cells, D ~114 cells, one owl redraw per source frame, and in E a 1/4-resolution blur.
  - The first frame builds the sōko page, its glow and the colour page (≈ 40 s here, under heavy machine load).
  - Per-frame times could not be measured reliably: the canvas work is deferred, so the harness reports 1–5 ms.
