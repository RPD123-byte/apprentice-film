## Known gaps (honest)

- **The designer is the largest error** (ΔE blocks of 12–20 around his head in 123–129). He is one drawing, of the 125.0 s view, moved by a measured mesh. His shapes are placed within about 5–15 px of the source, but the drawing is a flat, simplified cartoon:
  - The hair is a black mass with a handful of tapered locks, where the source has many fine strands.
  - The face has flat skin with painted blush and simple eye and nose strokes, without the source's hatching.
  - The jacket's folds are generic, and the arms and hand are simplified.
  - The source redraws him as the camera moves. The mesh and TPS bend the 125.0 s drawing instead, so in the entry (120.8–121.6) and in the end orbit (128.6–129.3) his pose drifts from the source by up to about 30–60 px. In the end orbit the source shows more of his arms and torso.
- **The owl** is a simple rig in the right palette: cream face disc, warm-grey cap and wings, big brown eyes, orange beak, one plaster, dark band, orange and yellow cups, orange feet. Compared with the source:
  - Its proportions are close, but it is less round and fluffy.
  - Its spread wings are simpler, with five blade feathers.
  - Its "turn" in the end orbit is a slide of the face features, not a redrawn three-quarter view.
  - On the screen it is the same rig, scaled down; it does not hop down the box tower.
  - The flight path is keyed every 0.04–0.16 s, so the wing beats are simplified.
