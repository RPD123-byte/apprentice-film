## What changed, with measurements

### Screen blur (review: high)
- The 8 ghost copies are gone. The blur is now a continuous horizontal box built by repeated doubling (2^k taps, each ≤1.25 canvas px apart; `boxBlur` in s01/screen.js).
- **Centring bug found and fixed.** Fix 1 pre-shifted the tape the wrong way, so every smear sat about one blur width off to one side.
- **Width is a per-frame table** (`LTAB`), not a formula. Frame-to-frame displacement does not predict the source's smear: 1.56 is smeared about 140 page units with only 0.08 of travel. The table was:
  1. seeded by a 2-D fit of each rectified source frame against the tape built from the source's own resting pages (lfit2d.py);
  2. then calibrated over three render passes against the source's sharpness in the screen box 880–1260 × 75–265.
- **Sharpness measure** (sharp.py): mean |dI/dx| after a σ 1.2 blur. The blur removes film grain; without it, grain dominates the reading.
- **Result:** render/source ratio over 0.56–4.52 is 0.71–1.14, mean 0.88. The resting Letters page itself sits at 0.75, because of page content rather than blur.
- **Onsets and settles follow the source.** It is smeared at the onsets (0.68, 1.56, 2.36, 2.88) and sharp at 3.08 and 4.36.
