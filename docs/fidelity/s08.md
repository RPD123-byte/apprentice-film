## Known gaps

- **Logo:** the top-left mark is still an original "Wonder / Camp" script, deliberately not the source's third-party wordmark. Its box keeps ~21 levels of blurred error until it leaves frame (~31.9 s). It is the largest remaining difference: without it the shot averages 2.94.
- **Keyline cores in the lower left and on the U's arms:** sampled at the source's line centres, they are still ~5–9 levels lighter than the source's (lobes and fan +9). Darkening them raised the blurred error in every region there, so they were left as they are.
- **Cloud under the card's bottom edge:** still ~5–8 R too red, in the card's shadow.
- **Late softness:** the ribbon (+0.11) and cloud (+0.09) are a touch soft at 32.4–32.56. The F0 blur has to balance the early and late frames.
- **Softness late in the push-in:** the source's page-art σ grows more slowly than the zoom (×1.28 by 32.5 s against the zoom's ×1.39), while the render's scales exactly with the zoom. The early frames now get an extra output-space blur (fix pass 2). Late, the render is 0.0–0.1 px softer than the source, and the cloud's outer edge is ~0.2 px softer.
- **Keyline depth:** keyline widths inside the r's stripes still vary against the source. Some minima are too dark (14 vs 22) and others too light (29 vs 17), because the gaps between the traced shapes are locally 1–2 px off.
- **Nub details:** the nub's dark-red diagonal and the sliver's colour are approximate. The nub box keeps a blurred error of ~5.
- **Selection drift:** the source's selection line jitters ±0.2–0.3 px against the camera. The static line matches the mean.
- **Residual placement:** a 0.1–0.35 px render-against-source pattern remains (F0's own placement).
- **Typed text:** set in Menlo with a paper-coloured erode. The "i"s carry ~15% less ink than the source's slab-footed "i".
- **Cloud body:** its colour varies by ±5–10 levels in patches. Around the sparkle, the body is ~8 levels darker than the source.
- **Pills:** the labels are original ("Tix & Info ( v )"), because the source's second label is unreadable.
- **Black level:** the crop strip grades to ~3 levels where the source's is ~1. The film grade sets that floor.
- **Compare-tool quirk:** at T = 30.56, tools/compare.sh renders 30.533 (s07) against source frame 764. At 32.56 it seeks 32.533, so the grain differs. Use multiples of 0.04 s from 30.60.
- **Browser caveat:** in the film's browser build, a curved `clip()` followed by a `multiply` draw draws nothing. The card light and the noise gain therefore avoid curved clips. The fix-pass-2 sharp redraw uses a curved clip with a plain source-over draw, which works: the film path is bit-identical to the harness.
