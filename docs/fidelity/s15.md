## What it is built from

`src/shots/s15.js` plus helpers in `src/shots/s15/`.

- **Scenery ("roto").** Every visible face of every source frame is a screen quad with four measured corner values, a stroke direction and a hatch gain (`roto{A,B,C,D,E,H,I,J}.js`, numbers only). `roto.js` back-projects the quads through the take's camera, and the corridor shader (`gl.js`) draws them with charcoal strokes, broken inked edges, handle slots and stacked far boxes, over a continuous 3D floor whose value comes from a measured 13×7 grid per frame.
- **Gear pictures.** Drawn in code into the texture atlas (`atlas.js`, `gear.js`) and mapped onto their faces.
- **Floor props.** Cable coils and the hanging cable (`overlay.js`).
- **Owls.** The back view is `owl.js`, posed from `sets.js`. The front and 3/4 views are `owlFront.js`, built on the owlInk `flyToward` rig with the head placed per frame from a template track.

## Known gaps (honest)

**Surface look.**
- The faces are shader strokes over measured tone, with bilinear four-corner values. The source is a dense hand drawing with tape seams, flaps, wood grain, and heavy dark lips on the box edges.
- The lit-face streaks are faint and generic.

**Scenery geometry.**
- The quads are flat faces picked by eye, so edges sit 5–20 px off in places. Sharp frames (J2) show it more: J2's gradient correlation is the lowest, 0.26.
- Several frames still have layout differences the review did not list: for example 44.72–44.84, where the left low boxes have their own shapes.
- The hanging cables at 42.40–42.56 are simple curves, not the source's ribbed rope drawing.

**Gear.**
- The pictures follow the source's layout and manner but are much simpler than its drawings.
- The take-H rack is laid out on one frame (44.08) and scales with its axis-aligned quad in the other frames.
- The J1 gear and low boxes still carry the largest error (σ12 23.8).

**Owls.**
- The back view has generic feathers and no pencil texture. The stride phase is procedural, not measured per frame. The head turns are approximated by cup placement and roll.
- The front and 3/4 views use the owlInk rig, with a rounder, simpler body than the source's.
- The review's size corrections were measured and not applied (see above). If a later reviewer measures the owls differently, the `SIZE34` table and the `k` / `wingK` parameters are ready.

**Floor.** Values come from the coarse measured grid, and the strokes are procedural.

**Speed.** The owl-front frames are over 300 ms.
