## Open decision (compliance, high; for the orchestrator or the user)

This pass did not change these:
- **`data/cel.js`.** The headphones round his neck are 12 cel levels at σ0.9, close to a posterised copy of the source crop. The prepared replacement is `trace/cel2.py`: one dark base, an 8-level σ3 study and measured centre-lines. Running it switches `paintCel` automatically. It measured worse: σ2 on the headphones 10.4–10.8 against 6.3.
- **`data/room.js` LEVELS.** The value study is 22 levels of the σ3.5-blurred plate. Cutting it to 8 or fewer would mean re-measuring the room (its calibration work files are gone), with a visible banding risk.
- **`data/mug.js` and `data/roomfix.js`.** Coarse grids of source tone.

All four are geometry and values measured from the source, not pixels. Whether their density breaks the rule against embedding source pixels is not mine to decide.
