## Method caveat (read first)

Nothing in the render is a pixel of the source, but the drawing is **measured from the source, not drawn independently**. Every plate and most owl drawings are *ink drawings*:
- **Lit faces:** one lit/dark threshold, as outlines with holes, each with a smooth fitted value field.
- **Strokes:** dark and bright stroke centre-lines, with a width at each point.
- **Specks:** a few small marks.

`src/shots/s18/trace2/*.py` measures these, and `s18/inkdraw.js` redraws them. This is the same class of data as s12's traced strokes and as s16, s19 and s20: closer to rotoscoping than to an original drawing. `owlB.js` alone is 1.3 MB.

Fix pass 4 added **hand-drawn parts** (`s18/handdraw.js`, `s18/data/feetA.js`). Their geometry was read by eye off zoomed, gridded source crops and checked by overlay; they are coordinates and shapes, not pixels:
- the shot-A feet (a small rig per foot, per frame);
- the shot-A beak;
- the shot-A inked outline;
- the knob caps across the lit slats in shot B;
- the key wedges that were missing;
- the covert-feather hooks at 1425;
- the speaker side panel's fine vertical grain (procedural, keeping the traced plate's local mean).

Fix pass 2 of review 2 added (details in its section below):
- the redrawn shot-A feet (`s18/feet2.js`, `s18/data/feetA2.js`): a parametric foot drawn by Canvas2D, whose per-frame parameters were **fitted by a pattern search against the source frame** (blurred error in the feet box), starting from the earlier eye-read placement. Only the parameters are stored;
- shot-A tail poses (and a feather spread) fitted the same way (`s18/tailA.js`);
- measured value-correction blobs (the `tone.js` form) for the ear cup (`s18/data/cushTone.js`) and for the key shadows at 1382–1383 (`SHADE_B` in `s18/shotB.js`).

Two alternatives are kept in the tree, unused:
- The hand-built fix pass 1 is in `s18/handbuilt/`.
- The level-set traced fix pass 2 is in `s18/traced_v2/`.

If the owner wants hand-authored drawings only, this shot needs redoing.
