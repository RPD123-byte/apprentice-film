## Pass 4: what changed and how it was measured

1. **The cut drawing is the source's drawing again.** The fix pass drew the owl along the cut (74.52–79.76) and in the walk (81.72–83.92) with s21's owl painter, posed to fit.

- **Scissors and toes.** The scissors' fine bevel lines and the toe claws are softer than in the source's single frames (see fix pass 1). The brandish's blade edges were added in fix pass 2.
- **The card (fix pass 2).** Its torn edge, crease and curl follow the measured per-pose statistics, not the source's exact rows: the crease sits in seeded rows, which costs ~1.5 σ1.5 in the edge strip at 83.60 against a clean edge. Its bright hairline is short (+0…+7 against +10…+13 at 83.6–84.0), because the card is at the pre-grade cap already.
- **Owl halo, fitted per side (fix pass 2).** The fit is per side, on 77.00–77.40 and 79.24–79.72. The top side and the late drawings' own light (OWN_LATE) were not refitted.
- **Cost.**
  - The room light is one full-frame copy and three full-frame composites. The owl's own light is composited over the margins only. The line layers are built once per sprite, on the CPU.
  - On the film's renderer (fix pass 2, load ~20–30 on 16 cores, all 238 frames): first frame 1.6 s, warm median 78 ms, p90 110 ms, max 306 ms (the frames that build a new sprite).
  - Renders are deterministic: the same frame is identical in any render order and across `compare.sh` runs.
- **Compliance question (open, for the user).** The owl drawings are vector traces of the source's own drawings: silhouettes, tone levels and line control points measured on source medians and frames. No pixels are stored. Fix pass 2 added more of the same kind: the haul drawing's feet outline and cup levels, and the walk and brandish strokes. Whether this is acceptable under the "never embed any frame/crop/pixel" rule is the user's decision. The alternative is posing the shared owlInk.js owl to the measured keyframes, which pass 4 found reads as a different bird.
