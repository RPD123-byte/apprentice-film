## Why this was rebuilt

The previous `s23.js` drew the owl and every effect as "cels": per-frame vector traces of the source frame's foreground, 8–11 nested flat-colour level outlines per frame for all 326 frames (`s23/cels.js`, 3.9 MB). That is the source footage stored in vector form, which the brief forbids ("never load or embed any frame/crop/pixel of the source"). F2 made the same call for the traced `hippie.js`.

This pass draws the owl with the owl kit and the effects procedurally. Only measurements are kept: positions, tracks, quads and segments.

Measured the same way, the traced version scored lower (9.0 over all 326 frames) than this drawn one (9.2 on every 2nd frame). The gap is small because most of the error is the page, not the owl.

Kept, not deleted (they were made in an earlier run):
- The previous files `s23/cels.js`, `celdraw.js`, `data.js`, `owl.js`, `fx.js` and `trace/` stay where they were. They are no longer imported.
- `s23.js` and this log as they were are backed up byte-for-byte (md5 checked) at `snapshots/s23r-prev/s23.traced.js` and `s23.traced.md`.

## Known gaps at the end of the build pass (superseded where the fix pass 1 section above says so)

- **The owl is the kit's cartoon, not the source's drawing.** The source draws a soft, engraved, detailed bird: fluffy feather texture, grey modelling, big expressive eyes and brows.
  - Ours is cleaner and flatter. The head is a smooth rounded shape, and the plaster, brows and cups are simplified.
  - Silhouettes land roughly on the source's (white-part IoU about 0.5–0.68 on the fitted frames). Faces and the 3/4 head turns while carrying are only approximate.
- **Wings are procedural feather fans.** They are spikier and more regular than the source's soft layered wings, and nothing is motion-blurred.
- **Animation is simplified.**
  - The source animates on twos: the body bobs, the wings flex and the head moves while it walks and carries.
  - Ours holds one drawing per beat, moved along the measured track, with only a 4-drawing foot cycle.
  - The hop, landing, wipe and crouch are single held drawings per beat.
- **The aura is a simpler vector flame** (one drawing, three boil variants). The source's hand-inked flames have many S-curved tongues, dark flame gaps and inner licks, and the flame mass shifts from beat to beat.
  - The error in 92.64–93.80 is 10.7 on average. It is highest (about 12) where the source's big ring and scraps change every frame.
- **The flying scissors (93.84–94.84) follow a made-up path**; they were not tracked per frame. The flare uses three stages, not the source's per-frame drawings.
- **The zip smears are soft bands** with a faint copy of the owl at the head. The source's smears are textured, directional motion blurs of the drawing.
  - 97.00–97.04 are the worst frames (15.5).
  - The confetti is seeded, not measured.
- **The page and banner are not mine.** F1's page (its illustration differs from the source's) is most of the error. F2's banner shows its own known differences in the header band.
- **Room light:** the side-margin gain (0.79 with the hole, 0.88 with the banner) and the top glow gain while carrying (1.18) are fitted by hand to pre-grade region means.
- **Performance:**
  - Owl drawings are cached per pose (built once at the origin and moved).
  - The aura, flare and pages are cached.
  - The per-frame cost is F1's `drawFramedPage` (~110 ms) plus blits. It was not timed precisely (the snapshot clock is frozen).
- **Determinism:** no `Math.random`, `Date` or `performance.now`. Re-renders are bit-identical (checked on 5 frames).
- **Motion check:** `tools/sidebyside.sh 84.04 97.08 4.5` in `snapshots/s23r-motion/`, 58 pairs. The beats land on the same frames as the source.
