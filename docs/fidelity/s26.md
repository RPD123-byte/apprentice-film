## Method (end fix round 1, still the basis)

The drawn build (the shared B&W owl kit posed per frame, straight-edged shard cells filled with pieces of our own pages, a painted colour owl) was replaced by traces of the source. Tracing scripts are in `snapshots/end-s26-fix1-tools/` (measurement only; nothing there is loaded by the film):

- `vtrace.py`: the tracer. It works on the ungraded frame (the film's grade is inverted with `gradeio.py`, so the grade can be applied on top again). It denoises, pulls out thin dark lines (black-hat) and thin bright lines (top-hat) as ink, and inpaints them away. The rest is clustered into colour regions (k-means in Lab over a shared palette per layer), and each region becomes a polygon with its mean colour. An underpaint of coarse regions of a blurred copy goes first, so dropped small regions leave the local average tone, not black. Ink polygons carry their measured tone.
- `owlmask.py`: the zones.
  - The white owl (C/D): the earlier silhouette masks, grown, plus the head disc, legs and measured ankles. The blurred left wing at 111.64–111.76 is added from the next frames.
  - The colour owl (F): the frame minus a clean page, which is the median of 17 late frames moved by the arrival camera. It is grown 3 px, plus weak differences within 48 px, which catches the headband over the navy.
  - The scissors: the tracked blades plus discs round the loops of this frame and the two before (the echoes).
  - D/E: "not the arriving page".
- `ztrace.py` (owls, scissors, per frame), `ckeys.py` + `ckeytrace.py` (C field keys), `dkeys.py` + `dkeytrace.py` (D ring keys), `etrace.py` (E per frame), `akeytrace.py` (A), `export_js.py` / `export_keys.py` + `codec.py` (polygon strings, the same codec as s27).
