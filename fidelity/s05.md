## Method

This is the drawn build from fix pass 1d, improved in fix pass 2. It is not the traced pass 3.

- **No traced data is loaded.** `data/settle.js` holds only the camera-motion grids.
- **Geometry is measured.** Each object is painted from control points read off labelled 2–6x grids of the rest frame (25.00). A few masses (the face, the owl, the hair behind the owl) use measured silhouettes, simplified to 2–4 px. A few key lines use ink centre-lines: the owl's wing rows, the finger contours and the hand's top edge.
- **Texture is authored.** Hair strands, knit, stubble, feathers, cloth tone and hatching are all drawn by code.
- **Light comes from coarse fitted fields.** These have knots every 40 px or more; the hair masses use 32 px. The desk top was re-fitted in this pass at 40 px, leaving out only the objects that really cover it.
- **The whole-frame light grid is clamped.** `tonegrid.js` was re-fitted from scratch, with its gains limited to 0.7–1.4 (it used to reach 2.5 and 0.44). About 2 % of its knots sit at the limit, listed under Remaining gaps.
- The measuring scripts are in `snapshots/s05-fix2-study/`. The film never loads them.
