## What fix pass 2 did

The rev2 review's defects are listed below with their status. Everything was measured on the source (row and column profiles, zooms, lit-mask overlays, σ 8 error maps, phase correlation) and checked with exact-frame renders. The scripts are in `snapshots/s13-fix2-tools/`. Where my measurements disagreed with the review, the measurement is stated.
