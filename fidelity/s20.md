## Known gaps (honest)

- **Owl drawing.** Still the kit's rounded bird with added parts, not the source's painterly, hand-inked owl. The source
  has heavier black contours, denser grey hatching and a longer side-view head. The grab crouch is the upright hold
  body rotated, not a true crouch: its feet do not land on the source's wide stance. The lunge body is rounder than the
  source's stretched one.
- **Whirl.** Each frame is still one of five drawings, blurred. The source redraws the owl every frame. The fans are
  stamped feathered wings. Their extents are measured, but their streak pattern and grey level only approximate the
  source. The whirl frames remain the worst frames of the segment (21–22 MAE).
- **Wings.** The new feathered wing is procedural, with the same feather layout everywhere. The hold mitten is a
  simple rounded mass with three fingers.
- **Dust.** Positions after the back-off are a compromise between the review's shift and the measured per-x light.
  The column's upper part is still a little dim (x 850: 32 vs 44).
- **Room.** Still a regular knife-line engraving, softened by the overlay rather than redrawn in the source's painterly
  hand. The side panel is ~6 levels darker than the source in A. The ~5 px shift of the panel in B2 is not reproduced.
- **Scissors.** The lean scissors' blades are narrower than the source's. The cobweb is a set of threads, not a
  strand-for-strand match.
- **Cuts (not s20).** The snapshot tool floors seek times to a 1/30 s grid, so 63.12 shows s19 in the harness. A
