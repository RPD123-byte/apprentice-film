**What changed and why.** The previous s25 (archived byte-for-byte in `snapshots/s25-prev/`) drew every owl, echo, burst
frame and the smoke from automated posterised vectorisations of source frames (value iso-contours at ~0.7 px with band
medians, ink centre-lines). That stores the source frames in vector form, against the brief's "never embed any
frame/crop/pixel" rule, so this pass rebuilt the shot from drawn parts. The old files in `src/shots/s25/` (tr_*.js,
timeline.js, smoke.js, fx.js, owlTrace.js, cracks.js, legacy/, trace/) were created in an earlier run and were left in
place per the user's instruction; `src/shots/s25.js` no longer imports any of them. Everything it uses is in
`src/shots/s25/v2/`.

**How it is built** (measuring scripts in `snapshots/s25-tools/`; nothing the film loads contains pixels)

- **Owl bodies** (`v2/data/*.js`, 23 standing poses + 10 chaos drawings, ~790 KB): for each distinct source pose, on one
  frame: the figure's outline (the earlier pass's measured outline ring), its major light areas (≥ 400 px²) and dark
  pieces as smoothed outlines with one linear tone plane each, the major ink lines as coarse polylines (≤ 220 per
  pose, ≥ 30 px long, 2 px simplification, one width and value each) and compact dark dots (`extract3.py`). Painted by
  `v2/draw.js` with a seeded feather-stroke texture and a paper grain. Placed per frame with the measured affines
  (`v2/sched.js`, from the earlier pass's ECC / template tracks).
- **Head** (103.08–110.48): the head plumage outline plus a hand-built face (bandage, brows, almond eyes with irises
  and catchlights, grin beak) and headphones (band, far cup, near cup) drawn from 4–10× grid measurements at 104.60
  (`v2/head.js`). One head layer per eye state (shut 103.08–103.52, half 103.56, sly after) is moved by the head's own
  ECC-tracked affine on every frame (`v2/headtrack.js`, cc 0.94–1.0).
- **Scissors**: a parametric model (blades, shanks, oval finger rings, screw; `v2/scis.js`) fitted per placement by
  chamfer + brightness (`scisfit.py`): the pairs held in the crouch / rise (moved by the measured tracks), and the two
  floating pairs (the spin 106.92–108.60 uses the earlier pass's measured pivot + rotation per frame). The pairs held
  at the hips from 103.96 and the huge shears are part of the pose drawings.
- **Smoke** (chaos): 13–40 rotated gaussian puffs per frame, fitted by least squares to the alpha of grey smoke over a
  clean chaos plate (owls, chips, slash arcs masked), inside the screen (`smokefit.py fit3`), broken up by a seeded
  wisp texture. Landing dust: the earlier pass's gaussian fits.
- **Cracks**: the earlier pass's measured straight-segment networks with every endpoint refined (±5 px) onto the
  median crack-ridge map of the source (mean ridge response 0.62 → 0.74; `crackfit.py`, `v2/cracks2.js`).
- **Light**: a 13 × 7 gain grid over the page fitted on 10 ungraded frames (`lightfit.py`), and the room glow gain per
  frame = the earlier pass's glow fit × a measured wall-brightness correction (source ÷ render round the screen).
- **Reused measurements (numbers only)** from the earlier pass: camera affines incl. the 108.08 squash, per-frame owl /
  echo affines, slash-arc ellipses, paper-chip rectangles, spin pivots, dust blobs, the glint reach, the wipe band,
  the negative frame's tone curve.
- **Burst** (102.64–102.96): the hover drawing with vertical ghosts, then the dive drawing re-placed per frame at the
  head position read off the source (1.35–1.45×, −11°), fading, inside the fitted smoke smear.

**Known gaps (honest)**

- **The owl is a reconstruction, not the source drawing.** Bodies are coarse measured shapes + major lines under a
  generic seeded texture; the added belly flicks, chest V and wing strokes are sparse and light next to the source's
  dense feather hatching and modelled shading; the talons stay simplified.
- **Chaos heads are one generic face.** Each chaos owl now reads (eyes, brows, bandage, beak, headphones) but it is the
  standing face remapped by three landmarks: the source's per-pose expressions (open beaks, angle-specific eyes) and
  exact head shapes differ; the band and far cup are simplified; the dash (101.84) head is squarer than the source's.
- **Echoes**: re-keyed on head positions but still built from the dive / top-right drawings; the left echo is a mirrored
  top-right drawing (its pose differs from the source's swept-back flying owl), and its smoke streak is short; the
  converging echoes and trails are a few levels too bright at 102.64.
- **Burst**: the ghost column and the smeared X are built from owl copies and a seeded streak texture — the look is
  close in layout and level (column levels within ~15 at most heights), but the source's grating-like texture, its
  huge overlapping owl shapes and the exact wing smears are approximated; the central owl is the dive drawing rotated.
- **Chaos smoke** stays as round fitted puffs (screen-blended): the source's long translucent diagonal streak along the
  dive is not reproduced (streaked puffs scored worse).
- **Scissors**: model shapes; the pop's two raised pairs are smaller and thinner than the source's; the spinning pairs
  are rigid with two faint trailing copies.
- **Chips** are per-frame detections, now kept off the owls and the arcs; a few chips on headline letters remain.
- **Cracks**: the refined measured networks; cracks that appear later in the source (a few after 105.6) are not added.
- **The page is F1's `sokoPageCanvas`** under a coarse light grid: its illustration differs from the source's
  everywhere (furniture, gear, the designer) — the largest remaining error in the standing phase; the floor under
  the owl is still 3–5 levels dark and the source's green cast in the darks is not reproduced.
- The negative frame (100.56) is a measured tone curve applied to our own frame; the surround is flat grey + vignette.
- Renders of the same frame in different browser processes differ by up to 9/255 on a few pixels (GPU raster/grade).
