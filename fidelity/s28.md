## Earlier pass (2026-09-27): what it did

The problem was that the hand-drawn rebuild looked clearly worse than the source. It is replaced by the traced build
(`archive/prev-builds/s28-prev/s28.traced.js`), which is now `src/shots/s28.js` again. That build's helpers were
still in `src/shots/s28/`, and it rendered with no API drift. The only change needed was that the end card now starts
at `CARD_IN` (135.72) instead of 135.78. The drawn build is kept, unused, in `src/shots/s28/drawn/`.

The restored build was then checked by eye against the source at viewing size, frame by frame. These fixes followed:

- **Orbit figure silhouettes re-measured.** In the old ones, the automatic outline took in patches of wall above the
  shoulder, bookshelf pieces and a lump of the chair. It cut the lit right edge of the beanie, which then floated
  loose from 130.24 to 130.40. It also dropped the elbow that the lid cuts off at 130.52, which left a notch showing
  the room.
  - Each of the 10 orbit keys was re-measured on its source frame and hand-checked with overlays. The method is an
    ink-bounded flood, then GrabCut, then local fixes: the drape by the chair and the owl at 129.32, the elbow piece at
    130.52, and a convex fill of the beanie's lit edge at 130.12 and 130.32.
  - The figure and room keys were then re-traced with the old trace settings.
  - The larger figure canvases get padded depth grids (`padGrid`). The key gains are resampled onto the new boxes.
- **Lid hole.** The top figure key's lid hole is now filled from both neighbouring keys:
  - the earlier key sees the strip left of the current lid;
  - the later key sees what the lid uncovers on its right.

  Before, the hole was filled from the second-nearest key only. After each key's own frame that is the later key,
  whose own lid covers the same strip, so a pale gap opened along the lid's left edge.
- **Mug.** It is now its own layer. Re-projected as part of the room, it tore apart and doubled from 129.36 to 129.88.
  Now one of 4 traces of it is moved by its measured track (template matching on the source, extrapolated as it leaves
  the frame).
- **Left edge, 129.32–129.84.** Here the bookshelf, chair and wainscot were stretched colour and doubled lines. Each
  source frame now has its own trace of the strip x < 330 (`leftpatch.js`), and the hoodie is drawn over it.
- **Bottom strip, 129.32–130.48.** The near desk, the papers, the cable and the laptop base were smeared and doubled,
  because that is where the parallax is largest, and it was the worst region of the frame (σ4 up to 17). Each source
  frame now has its own trace of y ≥ 836 (`botpatch.js`), with the figure and lid drawn over it. The figure is
  composited in a second GL pass, so these layers sit between the room and him.
- **Faint third ink level (fine hatching).** The old traces dropped the light hatching on the cheeks, the beanie
  knit, the hoodie folds and the wainscot, so skin read posterised. Every key and patch now carries it (`l = 2`, drawn
  under the two main ink levels).
- **Traced colours.** A few traced colours had a negative channel (e.g. `#3008-1`). Canvas ignores those and keeps the
  previous fill. They are now clamped in `trace.js`.
- **Gains.** The key gains (`keygain.js`) and the locked view's gain (`corr.js`) were refitted multiplicatively after
  the above. This is the only in-sample fitting: it used the key frames and the odd frames. Held-out even frames score
  the same as odd frames (3.72 / 2.08 against 3.71 / 2.06).
