## Fixed in pass 3

Each item was measured with an exact-time harness: `snapshots/s40-fix1c-h/` (`run.mjs` + `h.html` running the real post.js; scripts `grainstat.py`, `grainlum.py`, `dotstat.py`, `dotclass.py`, `shadowprof2.py`, `glyphfit.py`, `mad.py`). It was then checked in the real pipeline with `tools/compare.sh` and `tools/sidebyside.sh` (`snapshots/s40-fix1c-b`, `s40-fix1c-c`, `s40-fix1c-motion`, `s40-fix1c-motion2`).

**Moving grain (review: "texture mechanical")**
- **The problem.** post.js's grain is anisotropic. Its autocorrelation at 1/2/3 px is 0.59/0.40/0.31 along x but 0.43/0.01 along y, so at 1:1 it reads as horizontal streaks. The source's grain is isotropic 2–3 px blotches: 0.77/0.43/0.17 in both directions, and neutral (the same on R, G and B).
- **What the shot does now.** Once the card is up, `grade()` turns post.js's grain off. `grain.js` then draws the card's own grain every frame:
  - gaussian noise blurred at σ 1.1 px, shifted to a new offset every frame;
  - its gain follows the source's measured grain-vs-brightness curve;
  - it is added neutrally with an invert/add/invert/add composite (canvas has no subtract).
- **Result.** Autocorrelation is 0.81/0.43/0.15 in both x and y. Moving-grain std per brightness bin (50…250) matches within ±0.2 levels (source 2.57 / 3.81 / 4.31 / 3.66 / 2.66 / 1.58 at 50 / 90 / 130 / 170 / 210 / 250; render 2.57 / 3.85 / 4.33 / 3.52 / 2.82 / 1.73). Sky frame-difference std is 6.57 against the source's 6.53.
- **post.js's grain was not zero-mean.** On flat greys at 7.5/255 it darkens by 1.5–2.7 levels. Every colour on the card had been matched through that bias, so `grain.js` carries the same mean shift and the card's means stay on the source: sky −0.3 levels, cloud +0.0/+1.4/+1.9.
- **During the dissolve.** post.js's grain variance falls as the card's rises, so the cut shows no jump.

**Static print texture**
- **The problem.** A 12-frame mean shows that the source sky and letter faces carry no fine static texture. On the sky the leftover is ≈ the residual moving grain: fine std 1.06 against the render's 1.94 before this pass. The render's blocky 3.8 px value noise and its sky speckle are now kept to the clouds and ribbons.
- **Sky dot screen.** The source sky has a faint dot screen only in places: along the ribbons, at the top corners and low between the cloud banks. It is absent round the wordmark. Its strength was measured as band-pass rms on a 25×12 grid (`SKY_DOTS` in `data.js`) and is laid on at that strength.
