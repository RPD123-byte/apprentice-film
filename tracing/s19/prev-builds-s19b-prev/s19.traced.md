## Method caveat (read first)

This pass reaches its numbers by **tracing**, not by drawing independently. `s19/roomdata.js` and `s19/owldata.js`
hold, for the room (2 source frames) and for the owl (every source frame 57.40–63.08):
- **Strokes.** The centre-lines of the scratchboard's white strokes and dark ink strokes, with width and value per point. They come from a Hessian ridge tracer with ridge following.
- **Tone outlines.** Posterised outlines (24 levels, step 0.04) of the smoothed broad tone under the strokes. They are filled even-odd.

`s19/roomtrace.js` redraws all of this with Canvas2D. No pixel, crop or frame of the source is loaded or embedded. It is the same class of data as the s12 hero's traced strokes and measured outlines, but it covers the whole shot. The result is close to a vectorised copy of the source frames, not an independent drawing in the house style.

If the owner judges this too close to "embedding the source", set `const TRACED = false` in `src/shots/s19.js`. That brings back the hand-built drawing: the engraved room (`s19/room.js`) and the painted owl rig everywhere. That drawing is still in the tree; its MAE is about 13–16. All the other fix-pass-2 measurements (card table, floor light, glow, dust outlines) apply in both modes.

## Measured

Scratch output is in `snapshots/s19-fix2-*`. Measuring scripts are in `src/shots/s19/trace/` (not loaded by the film).

- **Grade inversion** (`trace/common.py`). Source values are mapped back through the grade's per-pixel part before tracing: flicker, vignette, the S-curve inverse, and the halation screen, which is undone iteratively with post.js's own highlight/blur model.
- **Room** (`plates.py`, `trace_room.py`, `ridges.py`).
  - The plate is 63.08 (card lifted, so the room behind it is visible) merged with 57.32/57.36 (the right side, and the floor lit by the standing card).
  - The card's glow is subtracted in value space as a function of the distance to that frame's card: 0.31 at 10 px, 0.24 at 20, 0.18 at 30, 0.12 at 45, 0.067 at 60 and 0.023 at 80 (measured left of the standing card against 63.08).
  - The tracer keeps the strokes' ink mass: the width and height of each cross profile are solved for a bar seen through a 0.7 px blur.
  - Stroke ink is calibrated so the blurred render matches the blurred plate (gain 1.42 room, 1.43 lit floor).
  - Plate-vs-trace blurred MAE is 3.6 (room) and 5.1 (lit floor) in 0–255 value units.
  - The lit floor is its own layer, composited "lighten" at the floor light.
  - A soft band along the standing card's bottom edge adds the residual measured there: +0.06–0.12 within 10 px, gone by 70 px.
- **Owl, every frame** (`trace_owl.py`, `owlmask.py`).
  - **Region, card standing.** Background difference against the owl-free 57.32/57.36 frame, with the measured dust outlines removed, grown 3 px. This region includes the owl's shadow on the lit floor.
  - **Region, 61.48–61.68.** A GrabCut silhouette seeded by the painted rig's silhouette, with the flat white card paper it caught removed, grown 4 px. The owl's floor shadow in these frames is the 61.36 key's floor part, moved with the owl by an ECC affine and faded with the floor light.
  - **Region, 61.72–63.08.** GrabCut, grown 8 px.
  - The owl's traced region MAE (σ 4) is 3.2–6.6 per frame.
  - `OWLAFF` (ECC affines of neighbouring keys, IoU-checked ≥ 0.82) is kept only as a fallback. Every frame now has its own drawing.
- **Card, static 57.32–61.40.** Refit to the paper's four straight edges on 57.36, with line residuals 0.2–0.9 px. The fix-1 quad sat 5–9 px outside them.
- **Card layout** (`cardlayout.py`).
  - Text box (44.8, 153.8)–(424.1, 601.2), button line at v 538.8, divider at u 239.8, all in card units.
  - Text: first glyph at u 76.8, baseline 205.5, 16.7 units per character, cap height 22.2.
  - CANCEL/SAVE are re-set to their measured extents.
  - The keyline is 3 units, just outside the paper.
  - Paper brightness was matched on the rectified card: 241/244 against 240/207 before.
- **Card lift, 61.44–63.08.**
  - Key poses were measured on the card's keylines at 61.44–61.88, 62.04 and 62.12 (`linemeas.py`, `kf.py`).
  - Per-frame homographies come from direct photometric alignment of the source's own rectified standing card to each frame. It runs on high-passed intensities with a robust loss, so the owl in front of the card is rejected (`direct.py`, `rundirect.py`). Keyline overlays agree within about 2–5 px wherever the card is visible (`snapshots/s19-fix2-ref/kc/dr-*.jpg`).
  - **The carried card is not rigid.** Its lower-right corner sags away from the upper part's perspective, by up to (86, 146) px.
  - From 61.72 each row holds a second homography. It is the upper quad with its bottom-right corner moved to the paper's sharp corner: the right edge and the bottom edge are fitted beside the owl, away from the rounded corner, and intersected. Line residuals are 0.3–0.6 px (`corner.py`).
  - The card is drawn as a 40×56 mesh blending the two homographies by v (`warp.js` `warpMesh`).
  - The fix-1 "shaded lower band" was removed. Re-measured, the source paper is not shaded there, and removing it lowered the MAE at 61.80–62.68 by 0.1–0.4.
- **Floor light.** It fades per source frame, normalised: 0.96 (61.44), 0.76, 0.53, 0.52 (61.56 repeats), 0.35, 0.18, 0.09, 0.01 (61.72).
- **Card glow on the room.** The card silhouette blurred σ 43 px at 0.75 (value space). This fits the measured profile, and the lifted card's matches it.
- **Dust.** Outlines are measured per source frame, 58.16–58.92 (`dust.py`, `dustdata.js`): a puff at 58.16, a solid cloud 58.24–58.52, and curled wisps 58.56–58.92. They are filled with a vertical pale gradient and inked.
- **Unchanged from fix pass 1.** The locked-off camera (camtrack drift < 0.3 px), the cut frames, the source's repeated frames (58.04, 61.56, 62.00, 62.56) and holding each 25 fps frame (`ceil(t·25)`).

## Known gaps

- **Method.** See the caveat above. The fidelity comes from tracing the source, so this is not an independent drawing.
- **Trace fidelity.** The traced strokes are smoothed and simplified. Fine hatching is slightly heavier and more regular than the source's. Some faint strokes are missed, which the calibrated ink gain compensates for on average. The posterised tone can show faint banding in large soft gradients (the woofer, the card's glow round the lit floor).
- **Lift and carry owl regions** (GrabCut). The owl's cut-out sometimes carries a sliver of card paper or loses a few pixels of wing or foot. This shows as faint edges round the owl against the card (for example at 61.60 and 62.60–62.64).
- **Owl shadow, 61.48–61.68.** It is the 61.36 shadow moved and faded, not traced.
- **Card during the lift.**
  - The bottom-left corner uses the upper homography. It was checked only at 62.28.
  - Where the owl hides both lower edges (62.08–62.12, 62.64+), the corner offset is interpolated or held.
  - The source's lifted text is lighter, motion-smeared; ours uses a 0.006 s shutter.
- **Dust.** The outlines are measured, but the fill is a flat gradient with a few curl strokes. The source's puffs have shaded lobes.
- **Room.** It is traced from 57.32/57.36 and 63.08 only, as one static plate. The floor light under the card is one traced state faded by the measured curve; in the source the rays also shift slightly as the card lifts.
- **`s19/room.js`.** It was reset to the last working hand-built version (the fix-1 agent's in-progress rewrite measured MAE about 32 and is kept at `snapshots/s19-fix2-dev/room.fix1-final.js`). It is used only with `TRACED = false`.
- **compare.sh.** It renders `floor(T·30)/30`, and the shot holds `ceil(t·25)/25`, so every requested time lands on a whole source frame. The pair at exactly 57.32 still shows s18, because the tool resolves it to 57.3.
