## Known gaps (honest)

- **Method.** The drawings are vector tone traces of the source frames. They are iso-value contours with a grey level each, the same approach as F2's hippie traces and s12's traced strokes, and no pixels are stored. They are nonetheless derived directly from the source frames rather than drawn from a model. The measuring scripts and their source-derived working arrays are kept outside the project (`s24/trace/README.md`).
- **Page beats (k7–19).**
  - The page under the owls is F1's `sokoPageCanvas`. It differs from the source's page in its props and engraving (left-side boxes and guitar case, the speakers), and this dominates the page-frame error.
  - The page was kept, not traced, so that it stays continuous with s21–s23 and s25.
  - The traced page owls and smoke are grey. The source shows a faint colour bleed from the banner through the smoke.
- **Fine texture is softened.** Posterising into 9–18 levels loses sub-level hatching and the drawing's paper texture. High-frequency energy in bright areas is about 60–70 % of the source's, and some thin ink strokes come out grey rather than black. The whirl drawings (7 levels, single frames) are coarser.
- **Speed lines.**
  - Each line is a straight bar with a grey ramp and square ends, softened by half-resolution rasterisation. The source's ends taper more softly.
  - The faint sub-threshold streaks are approximated by a flat +2/255 lift.
  - A few merged or split lines remain, visible as short missing or extra segments.
- **k19 and k20 are parametric fits.** They use a directional blur and logistic ramps, not traced drawings. The dark corner of k20 has streak structure that a ramp cannot follow.
- **Not changed here.**
  - The source's B&W frames carry a slight green cast (G ≈ +4 over R and B) and stronger fine grain than `post.js` produces. Both are global-grade questions, and the neighbouring shots are neutral too.
- **Timing.** Measured in headless Chrome on a heavily loaded machine (load average ~230). The steady draw is 20–40 ms for owl and line frames, 100–160 ms for page-hold frames, and 150–200 ms for frames with F1's `addScreenGlow` (k24–28 and k82–90).
  - Every frame is under 300 ms.
  - The first frame of a beat builds its caches. For k7 this is several seconds, including the shared sōko and hippie caches. For k91 it is about 5 s, including s25's first frame.
  - Cached layers are CPU canvases. Blitting a GPU-backed canvas measured about 400 ms per copy.
- **Data size.** `dataF.js` 584 KB, `dataHJ.js` 1.05 MB, `dataPage.js` 136 KB.
- **The k91 import.** The frame imports `s25.js` read-only, with a top-level await inside try/catch. If s25 changes its first frame, the colour corner above the band follows it.
