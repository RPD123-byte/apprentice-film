## Measured beats and how each is built (source frames, 25 fps; frame px)

Tools and scratch work are in `snapshots/s26b-tools` (harness render and pairing), `snapshots/s26b-meas` (all tracing and fitting) and `snapshots/s26b-meas/fit` (the rig fitter).

### A (110.52–111.52): the cracked page, un-squashed

- **The cut from s25.** s25 squashes its whole frame vertically by 0.9836 at 108.08. The source undoes that at the 110.52 cut: the affine to the pre-squash frame 107.88 is y′ = 1.0043·y − 3.7.
- **Camera.** The camera creeps out, zoom 1.0029 → 1.0 about (1150, 950), fitted from a per-frame ORB affine.
- **Cracks.** 708 straight segments (`s26/cracksA.js`), traced by s25's method: presence of thin bright lines over the 26 A frames minus the uncracked 90 s page, then Hough on the skeleton, collinear merging and endpoint snapping. The owl is masked out. Rays that leave the impact stars are drawn as wedges.
- **Impact stars.** Two outlines traced on the median A frame, at (645, 391) and (1522, 364), with a jagged black ring.
- **The owl.** s25's traced "hips2" drawing (`s26/poseA.js`, `s26/owlA.js`, copied from s25), un-squashed and refitted to the A owl's silhouette: sx 0.995, sy 0.985, +5 px. Silhouette IoU goes from 0.915 to 0.947. The cut is continuous with s25.
- **Floor.** A black floor under the feet; the source hides the page's figure there.
- **The stuck scissors.** Pivots (772, 477) and (1395, 453), blades into the stars, and finger-loop centres measured on 3× crops. They use s21's scissors design (`s26/scissorsFlung.js`).

### B (111.56): the jolt

- The page is thrown up and shrunk: x′ = 0.9911x − 8.5, y′ = 0.9905y − 19.6 (ORB, 926 inliers). The room glow is off.

### C (111.60–112.68): the blast

- **The shard field** (`s26/fieldC.js`).
  - **Outlines.** Bright thin lines present in most frames of 111.84–112.68, once each frame is mapped back onto 112.00 by its ORB affine. 952 Hough segments.
  - **Cells.** The 439 cells between the outlines. Each is labelled by what it shows on 112.00:
    - B&W page, under a global similarity of the stage page plus the cell's own shift (masked NCC, up to ±150 px);
    - colour page, under the headline similarity s 0.846 plus a shift up to ±170 px (the headline pieces thrown up);
    - flat dark, with its mean colour.
  - **Motion.** The field moves under its measured per-frame affine for 111.60–112.68. It is ~0.988 × 1.015 before the 111.80 shake frame, then a push-in to 1.048 by 112.68.
- **Colour page behind.** The colour page stays fixed behind the field.
- **The 111.80 shake frame.** A 0.45 ghost at (−50, −120), as before.
- **Speed lines.** Traced per frame from the bright thin-line mask with the outlines and the owl removed (`s26/fx.js` SPEED), and drawn as tapered wedges.
- **Flung scissors.** Both pairs' finger-loop holes (≈ 52 × 82 px ellipses) were tracked per frame. The pivot, blade angle and length are derived from them (`fx.js` SC_KEYS), with a faint 2-frame echo.
- **The owl** (`s26/rig.js`, poses in `s26/posesC.js`).
  - A procedural rig: head with tufts, headphones, plaster and almond eyes with asymmetric brows; pear body; "angel" wings; feet.
  - It is posed per source frame by a pattern-search fit of its silhouette to the source owl's mask (1/2 res, IoU 0.84–0.88).
  - The pose is anchored by both tracked irises. Head size and angle come from the pupil pair.
  - Eyes are squeezed shut 112.64–112.68.

### D (112.72–113.88): the ring and the hovering owl

- **Colour page.** It arrives on F2's `pageArrival`.
- **The ring** (`s26/ringD.js`).
  - **Cells.** 86 cells traced at 112.72 between outlines, which persist over 112.72–113.52 once each frame is mapped back by the ring's chained motion.
  - **Content.** 75 cells carry a measured page similarity: masked NCC over scale 1.5–2.35 and rotation ±24° against the median A page with its cracks inpainted; one colourful cell matched better against the colour page. The other 11 are flat grey.
  - **Motion.** The ring's own zoom was measured frame to frame (ORB on the left and right halves, chained): 1.09 by 113.28, 1.33 by 113.68. Past 113.68 it is extrapolated, accelerating about the measured fixed point (1091, 424).
  - **Leaked regions.** The rest of the ring is a stand-in: the page tiling (`s26/shards.js`) under the same motion, outside the measured colour window.
- **The owl** (`s26/posesD.js`, silhouette IoU 0.75–0.84).
  - Head turned to the viewer's right, eyes looking right.
  - Winking, with its right eye shut, 112.72–113.04. Half-open at 113.08, then open.
- **Scissors.** They leave through the top corners by 113.68.

### E (113.72–114.12): the lens shard

- **Geometry.** A 1250 × 1500 slab at −20 to −32°, centre per frame from the previous builder's fits (IoU 0.74–0.93). A refit on my own mask measured worse, so it was not used.
- **Texture.** Procedural, blurred hard. Two alternatives measured worse (σ8 ΔE +2 to +6), so the original was kept:
  - a retuned texture with streaks along the slab;
  - a blurred crop of our own page.
- **Behind the slab.** The owl switches to the colour owl, first seen at 114.08.

## Known gaps (honest)

- **The owls are procedural.**
  - **The white owl in C/D** has smooth grey-shaded feathers and a fluffy egg body. The source has dense ink hatching, a heavier head outline and a larger hooked beak. The wing feather layout is generic, fitted to the outline only.
  - **The colour owl** is flat vector shading. The source is painted and textured.
  - **Face.** It uses a fixed parametric layout. The big 3/4 owl at 114.1–115.3 is drawn frontal, with a feature shift for the turn. Its expressions are close but not the source's lines.
- **Silhouettes.** Silhouette IoU is 0.75–0.90. The worst frames are D (the raised wing) and the fast wing strokes in F.
- **C shard field.**
  - It is one traced field moved rigidly. The source's individual shards also tumble slightly.
  - The regions where the outline tracing leaked are split per pixel into colour page or B&W page with no shift, so some pieces there are wrong.
  - A few upper shards that move independently are missing.
- **D ring.**
  - Traced cells cover about 45 % of the ring (0.6 of 1.35 Mpx at 112.72). The rest is the tiling stand-in.
  - After 113.68 the motion is extrapolated. The source keeps a few shards on the right until ~114.1; ours clear earlier. A slower right half measured worse.
  - The source's dark ghost of a wing inside the right-hand shards at ~113.0 is not reproduced.
- **E slab.** The texture is procedural (streaks and blocks). The source shows out-of-focus gear, lighter and with more mist. This beat has the highest error.
- **Speed lines.** Traced per frame, but thin or faint ones are missed. Widths are estimates.
- **Scissors.** The stuck and flung pairs use s21's design, placed on the measured pivots and loops. The blade angle is derived from the loops; in D the upper right loop leaves the frame and its angle is extrapolated.
- **Page art.** The page art (the sōko page, the banner and the colour page) is F1's and F2's, and their gaps show through.
- **Cost.** Not measured, because the snapshot clock is frozen. Per frame:
  - A/B: one cached blit.
  - C: about 440 pattern-filled paths, plus the speed lines, the scissors and one rig owl (cached per source frame).
  - D: about 480 tiling paths, 86 cells and one rig owl.
  - F: the page blit and the rig.
  - First use builds the sōko and colour pages and the glowed framed page.
