## How it is reproduced (this pass)

- **Room:** traced from the source as posterized flat-colour regions with measured colours plus ink polygons, redrawn with canvas paths. Each trace is taken from a grain-free median of the frames around its time, un-graded, with our own foreground objects cut out and the holes filled from the surrounding colours.
  - 17 views are traced: 119.6, 125.0, 129.28 and 14 keys between 120.6 and 129.12.
  - Each view rides the measured wall homography relative to its own frame.
  - Neighbouring views cross-fade: the later view is laid under, then the earlier one, then the later one again at the fade weight.
- **Designer:**
  - He is traced from a registered 15-frame median at 125.0 and at 7 key frames of the end orbit.
  - He is warped by a mesh tracked on the source by template alignment. That is DIS flow of the mesh-warped reference against each frame, with a robust affine plus a damped non-rigid residual and no folds.
  - After 127.6 the mesh is chained by his own flow and refined key to key. The key views cross-fade.
  - Before 121.6 he still uses the chamfer-fitted similarity from the last pass.
- **Owl:** traced on every source frame 125.40–129.00 inside a measured silhouette.
  - The silhouettes come from background subtraction during the hold, then from GrabCut seeded by the previous frame's mask warped by ECC, cut at the measured lid edge.
  - The owl stays clipped to the display while it is inside the page. For 129.04–129.28 the 129.00 drawing moves by the face's template-matched drift.
- **Owl shadow:** measured each frame 126.44–127.6 in the base's own cm coordinates and drawn as a soft warm veil, so it rides the base in the orbit.
- **Laptop:** the keyboard is repainted: dark keys with glints on a silver deck, and a space bar. The side ports are added (SD slot and two ports, measured at 125.0). The base is thicker (17 px at 119.6 → 24 px at 125) and the display black is corrected.
- **Whip:** blur amount 0.17 with a 0.5 floor, so the centre is soft as in the source.
- **Lid:** the screen side is kept until 128.63, to match the source's sliver at 128.60.
- **Determinism:** identical pixels in any render order (max difference 0, checked twice).
- **Speed:** about 300 ms per frame including PNG encode; the first frame is about 470 ms. Load time is about 6–8 s, since the polygons are compact-encoded (about 5 MB of data in `s27/`).

## Known gaps (honest)

- **Method:** the room, the designer and the owl are now vector traces of the source, not hand drawings. They are posterized colour regions (about 1,500–2,200 per room view, about 400 per owl frame) plus ink polygons with measured tones, all redrawn by canvas paths. No source pixels are embedded, but at screen size they read as a posterized copy of the source's drawing, not as a reinterpretation. hippie.js and the earlier designer trace work the same way. Flag this if that is not the intended level.
- **Trace artefacts:**
  - Gradients are cut into flat bands.
  - Detail under about 2 px is lost (fine ink hatching, paper grain).
  - Ink lines are slightly crisper and heavier than the source's soft ones.
  - Where our cut-out masks leaked, a few owl frames around 128.2–128.9 carry a traced sliver of the room (mug, boxes) beside the owl. It is the true background colour in the right place, so it barely shows.
- **Room between keys:** views 0.4–0.6 s apart cross-fade. Where the source morphs fast, 121.2–123.6, the mid-points show a faint double image of shelf edges and plants, 5–20 px apart. The hand-painted underlay shows only at the frame edges during the turn.
- **Whip (117.52–117.56):** our radial streaks are smoother and less chromatic than the source's smeared, colour-fringed streaks, and its dark top band is not matched exactly. At ΔE ~10 these are the worst frames in the segment.
- **Lid edge-on (128.44–128.8):** interpolated between the screen homography and the fitted back outline. The 128.60 sliver of screen is thinner than the source's, and the back's shading is a flat gradient. The mug is interpolated while it is behind the lid (128.2–129.1).
- **Designer:**
  - During his fast entry (120.7–121.6) he is the 125.0 trace moved by a similarity, so he does not morph with the source. His outline is 5–15 px off there.
  - Between key views at the end, a cross-fade stands in for the source redrawing him.
- **Owl, 129.04–129.28:** the 129.00 drawing moved by the measured face drift. The source turns it further into profile there.
- **Base:** the base corners after 127.64 are hand keyframes. The notebook and pencil follow keyframes to within ~10–20 px between keys.
