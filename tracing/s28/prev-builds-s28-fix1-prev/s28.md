**Why this was rebuilt.** The previous s28 re-projected 34 dense automatic vector traces of source frames and added colour-gain grids fitted to the source. That is the source frame stored in vector form, which the hard rule forbids ("never load or embed any frame/crop/pixel of the source"). Its own log flagged the same concern. The F2 foundation owner reached the same conclusion for hippie.js.

This pass replaces it with drawn code in `src/shots/s28/drawn/`. `src/shots/s28.js` now only re-exports that shot.

Nothing from the earlier runs was deleted:
- The old `s28.js` and its log are kept byte-for-byte (md5 checked) in `snapshots/s28-prev/s28.traced.js` and `s28.traced.md`.
- The old helpers are still in `src/shots/s28/` (`keys/`, `gl.js`, `resid.js`, `keygain.js`, `corr.js`, `depth.js`, …). They are no longer imported. The user may want to delete them, since the key traces are the source-derived data.

**What is measured and what is drawn.**
- **Measurements carried over** (numbers only, re-checked this pass with `snapshots/s28d-tools/chkplane.py` and `chklid.py`), in `drawn/measure.js`:
  - the orbit camera (θ/pan/tilt per frame, f = 2651 px);
  - the lid's four corners per frame;
  - the breathing displacement per frame.
- **Measured this pass** (`snapshots/s28d-tools/`):
  - Outlines of the designer's big regions as simplified polygons, from colour-class regions (`region.py`) and ink-ridge following (`snap.py`): silhouette, hair mass (with the larger skin gaps between strands), face skin, forearm, hand, sleeve, beanie, the hood's top, fold lines, eye lids, mouth and nose. These are measured on the locked view (131.60) and on two orbit keys (129.32, 129.96), stored in `figLdata.js` and `keysData.js`.
  - The figure's own motion for each key: a homography plus a 9×6 residual grid per frame, from forward/backward-checked KLT tracks (`figtrack.py`), in `tracks.js`.
  - The lid face's bilinear colour field over time (`lidcol.py`).
  - Pre-grade colours of every surface, sampled from an ungraded estimate of the source.
- **Drawn by hand in code:**
  - The locked room (`roomL.js`): bookshelf, plant cabinet, back wall with the sun patch, the right wall with curtain, window, wainscot, cabinet, canister, plant and bin, the desk and papers.
  - The parts only the orbit shows (`roomX.js`): pot shelf, upper shelf with boxes and books, speakers, sideboard, desk views, laptop base, chair and mug. Positions were read off 129.32 / 129.96 and mapped between frames through the measured wall planes.
  - Every fill, shading, hatching and fold, the face, the eyes (with the open→shut morph), the headphones, the owl's peek, and the sun shafts.

**Known gaps (visible):**
- **Drawing detail.** The source is a dense illustration, with fine hatching on every surface, detailed hair strands, cheek blush lines, and many small props on the shelves. This build is flatter, cleaner and paler. The σ4 error stays at about 14.
- **Designer.**
  - The hair is a flat black mass with lighter streaks, cut from its measured outline, so it reads as blobby, particularly on the two orbit keys.
  - The face, lids and mouth sit on measured lines but are simpler.
  - The hands and fingers are approximate.
  - The hoodie folds are placed from the source's lines, but the soft shading is generic.
- **Orbit hand-overs.** The figure changes drawing at 129.60–129.72 (A→B) and 130.28–130.36 (B→L).
  - Each key is moved by the figure's measured motion (median error about 3–5 px), but the drawings differ in shape, so each hand-over shows 1–2 frames of soft ghosting.
  - Between 129.72 and 129.97 a vertical seam can show where key B's lid-hidden area is filled from key A.
- **Orbit room.**
  - The room is re-projected as three planes (back wall, window wall, desk). The chair, mug and laptop base are 2D keyframed props.
  - The left bookcase seen at 129.3–129.6 and the window frame seen at 130.3–130.8 are only roughly drawn or missing.
  - The desk uses three cross-faded drawings, because the source's desk is not rigid.
- **Light.** The sun shafts and patches are placed from measurements of the locked frame, but their soft falloff and the source's floating dust are simplified. The upper-left shelf area and the right cabinet in the shaft are the largest remaining tone errors.
- **Grain and texture.** Only the global grade's grain is used, with no paper texture.
